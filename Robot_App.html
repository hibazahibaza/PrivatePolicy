Privacy Policy for Robot Watch App

Effective Date: September 13, 2026

Thank you for using Robot Watch App. This privacy policy explains how the application handles information and permissions when you use the app.

1. Information Collection and Use

Robot Watch App is designed to operate primarily on the user's Wear OS device. We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit personal information to our own servers.

The application may access certain information and device sensors locally to provide its features. This information is used only to operate the application and is not transmitted to us.

Calendar Information

The application may request access to the device's calendar so that the Robot can display the user's calendar appointments and scheduled events. Calendar information is used locally by the application for this functionality and is not transmitted to our servers or sold to third parties.

Activity Recognition

The application may use the ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION permission to detect relevant user activity so that the Robot can respond or behave accordingly. Activity information is used for the app's functionality and is not transmitted to our servers.

Body Sensors

The application may use the BODY_SENSORS permission to access supported sensor information when required for the Robot's functionality. Sensor information is processed locally on the device and is not transmitted to our servers.

System Time

The application accesses the device's system date and time to display the correct date and time within the application.

2. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or share users' personal information with third parties.

Robot Watch App does not use advertising networks, analytics services, or cloud-based services to collect user information, unless otherwise stated in a future update to this privacy policy.

Information accessed through Calendar, Activity Recognition, or Body Sensors permissions is used for the functionality of the application and is not transmitted to our servers.

3. Permissions

Robot Watch App may request the following permissions, depending on the features available in the application:

  • Calendar: Used to access calendar events and appointments so the Robot can display or respond to scheduled events.
  • Activity Recognition: Used to detect relevant physical activity so the Robot can respond accordingly.
  • Body Sensors: Used to access supported body sensor information when required for the application's functionality.
  • System Date and Time: Used to display the correct date and time.

The application does not intentionally access location, contacts, microphone, camera, or other sensitive information unless such access is explicitly required and disclosed in a future version of the app.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the application. Because Robot Watch App does not transmit users' personal information to our servers, we do not maintain a server-side database containing users' personal information.

Users can control or revoke application permissions through the permission settings available on their Wear OS device.

5. Children's Privacy

Robot Watch App is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Because the application does not collect or transmit personal information to our servers, we do not knowingly maintain personal information belonging to children.

6. Third-Party Services

Robot Watch App does not currently use third-party advertising, analytics, or cloud data-storage services to collect personal information.

The application may interact with services or functionality provided by the Wear OS or Android operating system, such as the device calendar and sensors. These services are governed by their respective privacy policies.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application or its features.

When we make changes, we will update the Effective Date shown at the top of this Privacy Policy. We encourage users to review this page periodically for any updates.

8. Disclaimer

Robot Watch App features may change or be updated over time. The application is provided for its intended functionality, and users should review the permissions requested by the application and the available device settings.
